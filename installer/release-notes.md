**Download `geoguessr-AI-Setup.exe` below and run it.** It needs Windows 10 or 11, and no administrator.

Windows may warn that it doesn't recognise the installer, as it isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

It installs the app, and fetches about 1 GB of libraries. The app then offers to download the model, about 2.2 GB. To play on a hidden screen, so your mouse stays yours, it sets that up too, with another 1.5 GB or so in WSL.

Only play it against friends who know it's a bot, never in ranked or public games.
