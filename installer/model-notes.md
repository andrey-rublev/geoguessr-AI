The model `geoguessr-ai fetch-model` and the app download: not for running by hand.

It holds the trained classifier and embeddings of 250,000 street photos and about 4,000 played rounds, as numbers: no pictures. It sees through StreetCLIP, which is downloaded from Hugging Face separately.

Embeddings of OpenStreetView-5M photos (CC-BY-SA 4.0), through StreetCLIP (CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial use only).
